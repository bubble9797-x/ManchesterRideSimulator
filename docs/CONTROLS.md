Menus: D-pad / arrows / WASD to move, A / Enter / Space to confirm, B / Esc / Backspace to go back.

## Riding tips

- **Casual wheelies:** hold the wheelie button while riding at any speed. The
  balance assist finds the balance point; lean back or forward to fine-tune; let go
  to come down. Assist modes are in Settings → Wheelie / stunt assist.
- **Manuals:** wheelie on a bicycle without pedalling. They score more per metre.
- **Dirt bikes and motorcycles:** in Balanced or Realistic mode, wheelies come from
  the throttle in low gears. Weak bikes (125s) need a clutch dump: hold the clutch,
  rev, release.
- **Stoppie:** hard front brake while leaning forward.
- **Slides:** rear brake plus steer, or wheelspin on loose ground.
- **Spins and flips:** steer and lean while airborne. With landing assist on, let go
  before landing and the bike lines up.
- **Wet roads:** grip falls as rain soaks the road. Wet cobbles (setts) are the
  slipperiest surface.
